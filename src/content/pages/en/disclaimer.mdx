## Information, not an official alert

SismoAtlas is an information website. **It is not an alert service** and does not replace the authorities. In the event of an earthquake, follow the instructions of the Moroccan authorities and Civil Protection.

## Data may be revised

Recent earthquakes are displayed automatically from public data (USGS, EMSC, IGN). In the first minutes, magnitude, location and depth are **preliminary**: agencies may revise them, sometimes significantly.

Simplified descriptions ("18 km north of…", "shallow") are computed automatically and remain approximate.

## No forecasts

Nobody can currently predict the date, place and magnitude of an earthquake. SismoAtlas publishes **no forecasts**. Comparisons with "usual activity" describe the past, not the future.

## Limitation of liability

Information is provided as is, without any guarantee of accuracy or completeness. SismoAtlas cannot be held liable for decisions made on the basis of this information.
