## Study area

The "Morocco and surroundings" area spans latitudes 20° N to 38° N and longitudes 18° W to 1° E: the whole of Morocco, the Alboran Sea, southern Spain and Portugal, and western Algeria.

## Two complementary catalogues

**1. Reference catalogue, 1045–2005.** It is based on the catalogue by Peláez et al. (2007), which compiles historical and instrumental earthquakes in the region (27° N–37° N, 15° W–1° E). Its magnitudes have been unified as moment magnitude (Mw). It is **declustered**: it contains only mainshocks (no foreshocks or aftershocks), identified with the Gardner and Knopoff (1974) method, and only events with Mw ≥ 3.0.

**2. Seismicity since 2006.** It comes from the online catalogues of IGN (Spain), USGS and EMSC. It contains **all** detected earthquakes, including aftershocks, with different magnitude types (Mw, mb, mbLg, ML…).

The two datasets are not directly comparable: they are shown as two separate layers, and statistics are computed only within the same dataset.

## Sources

- **IGN** – Instituto Geográfico Nacional (Spain): earthquake catalogue with good coverage of northern Morocco and the Alboran Sea.
- **USGS** – United States Geological Survey: global catalogue, PAGER impact levels, tsunami alerts.
- **EMSC** – European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre: catalogue, real-time feed and testimonies.
- **Peláez et al. (2007)**: reference catalogue 1045–2005.

## Data limitations

- The number of small earthquakes detected rises sharply after 2010 because station networks became denser: this does not reflect increased activity.
- Old locations are imprecise (sometimes by several tens of kilometres).
- Recent magnitudes of different types are not all equivalent to Mw.
