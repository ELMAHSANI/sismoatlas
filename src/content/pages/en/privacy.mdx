## Our commitment

SismoAtlas complies with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data. The site is designed to collect as little data as possible.

## Data collected

- **No account, no form**: the site does not ask for any personal data.
- **No tracking or advertising cookies.**
- **Audience measurement**: we plan to use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a tool that works without cookies or profiling. [À COMPLÉTER : confirm the tool and its configuration at launch]
- **Preferences**: your theme choice (light or dark) is stored only in your browser. It is never sent anywhere.

## External services

- **"Did you feel it?"**: this button takes you to the testimony form of the EMSC (European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre). Data you enter there are processed by the EMSC under its own privacy policy.
- **WhatsApp sharing**: sharing goes through the WhatsApp app, under its terms.
- **Base map**: map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap (data © OpenStreetMap), which may receive your IP address. The map is only loaded when you display it.
- **Live earthquakes**: your browser connects to the EMSC public feed (seismicportal.eu) to receive new earthquakes; the EMSC may receive your IP address. SismoAtlas sends it no personal data.
