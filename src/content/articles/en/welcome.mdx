## A site to understand earthquakes in Morocco

SismoAtlas presents earthquake activity in Morocco, with a focus on the Rif region, and follows major earthquakes worldwide. It is written for both the general public and the scientific community.

## What you will find here

- recent earthquakes in Morocco and neighbouring regions, explained simply;
- pages on the major earthquakes that shaped the country;
- explainers and a safety guide;
- the sources and methods used.

## Who are we?

SismoAtlas is an independent, non-commercial initiative. Our goal: to give everyone **easy, real-time tracking of earthquake activity** in Morocco and worldwide, along with **reliable information** to understand earthquakes and be prepared.

In practice, we provide:

- live earthquake tracking, explained in plain language (location, Moroccan time, magnitude, depth);
- pages on major earthquakes, explainers and a safety guide that works offline;
- data from recognised agencies (EMSC, USGS, IGN), with their sources and our methods.

SismoAtlas provides information but does not replace the authorities: in an emergency, follow official instructions.
