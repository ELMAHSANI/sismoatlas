## A site to understand earthquakes in Morocco

SismoAtlas presents earthquake activity in Morocco, with a focus on the Rif region, and follows major earthquakes worldwide. It is written for both the general public and the scientific community.

## What you will find here

- recent earthquakes in Morocco and neighbouring regions, explained simply;
- pages on the major earthquakes that shaped the country;
- explainers and a safety guide;
- the sources and methods used.
